============================================================

Selection metric: standardized mean gap |mean_A - mean_B| / pooled_std,
before and after each candidate transform. Lower is better (0 = means
coincide in pooled-std units). NOT selected by KS statistic -- KS is
invariant under any monotonic transform applied identically to both
datasets, so it cannot distinguish log1p/z-score/robust from doing
nothing; it's reported alongside for reference only, not as a
selection criterion. Note: tree-based/isolation-based detection
models (Ch6) are scale-invariant and are trained separately per
dataset, so this remedy applies to the CROSS-DATASET COMPARISON/
VISUALIZATION step (this chapter), not to the detection models
themselves.
